# Personas

## Production Planner
Plans what gets built and when. Creates Production Orders, checks
Material Requirements against stock, and schedules work across Work
Centers.

## Work-Center Operator
Executes the work. Starts and logs a Production Run at their assigned
Work Center, records actual output, and reports any Downtime Event.

## Quality Inspector
Reviews output before it's released. Records Quality Inspection results
(pass/fail) against a Production Run, and determines whether output
becomes a Finished Good.
